# Delete the small talk

## Purpose

Make the first-message problem visible and resolve it simply.

## Audience

Daters who want to connect but overthink how to begin.

## Story

A typed opener is deleted three times before the app supplies a specific playful prompt.

## 15-second script

**0.0–3.0s** Type. Delete. Type. Delete.

**3.0–7.0s** A conversation card appears.

**7.0–12.0s** The recipient laughs and replies.

**12.0–15.0s** *Skip the small talk. Try Example Dating.*
