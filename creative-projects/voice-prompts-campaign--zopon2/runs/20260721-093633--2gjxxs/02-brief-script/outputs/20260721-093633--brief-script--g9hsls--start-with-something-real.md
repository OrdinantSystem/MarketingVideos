# Start with something real

## Purpose

Show that voice prompts help conversations begin with personality instead of repetition.

## Audience

Thoughtful urban daters aged 25–38 who are tired of generic small talk.

## Story

Mara sees the same opener three times, discovers a funny voice prompt, and responds with genuine curiosity.

## 15-second script

**0.0–1.5s — Hook**  
Three “Hey :)” messages stack. Caption: *When every chat starts the same…*

**1.5–4.0s — Friction**  
Mara exhales and closes the chat.

**4.0–8.0s — Product**  
A voice prompt appears: *My most irrational opinion is…* A playful answer begins.

**8.0–12.0s — Payoff**  
Mara smiles and sends a real response.

**12.0–15.0s — CTA**  
Logo and line: *Start with something real.*
